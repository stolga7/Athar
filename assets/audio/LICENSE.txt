ATHAR — audio asset provenance
==============================

Every .wav file in this folder was generated by this project's own tool:

    tools/generate_audio.py

They are synthesised from sine waves and filtered noise. No recording, sample
library, instrument, or third-party sound was used, copied, or downloaded, so
there is no third-party copyright or licence attached to any of them.

Layers:
    ambience_day.wav    wind in leaves, four distant birds
    ambience_night.wav  still air, two cricket voices
    rain_loop.wav       steady shower with individual droplets
    music_ambient.wav   slow three-chord drone pad, no melody or percussion
    step_grass_1-3.wav  hunter footsteps, forest floor
    step_soil_1-2.wav   hunter footsteps, bare earth
    step_stone_1-2.wav  hunter footsteps, ruined stone courtyard
    hoof_1-2.wav        deer hooves
    deer_alert.wav      warning snort
    deer_flee.wav       snort followed by hoofbeats
    capture.wav         capture confirmation chime
    ui_tap.wav          interface tap

Permission: these files are original works created for ATHAR and may be used,
modified and redistributed with this project. No attribution is required.

Reproducibility: the generator uses a fixed seed and no external input, so
re-running `python3 tools/generate_audio.py` rewrites byte-identical files.
Totals: 17 files, about 1.1 MB, 16-bit mono PCM at 16 kHz (ambience, music) or
22.05 kHz (effects).
